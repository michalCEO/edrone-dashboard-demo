Screen 3: Store vs Product Reviews Picker

Modal for choosing review type when adding the Best Opinions widget

Current

What will help this shopper buy?

Choose the reviews that fit the job best here: build trust in your store or bring the shopper back to a specific product.

Store reviews

Best when you want to build trust in the store, the brand, and the whole shopping experience before the first purchase.

They show that buying from your store feels like a safe and proven choice.
They give first-time shoppers more reasons to trust your brand before they buy.

Product reviews

Best when you want to bring shoppers back to one specific product and help them make the final decision faster.

They bring the shopper back to the exact product they were already considering.
They help remove the last doubts and move the shopper closer to purchase.
Rewrite

What will help this shopper buy?

Two kinds of proof. Pick the one that matches the moment.

Store reviews

Build trust in your store — For first-time visitors who don't know you yet.

"Is this store legit?" — Real reviews answer that before you have to.

Best for: welcome emails, new subscriber flows, brand-building messages.

Product reviews

Remove doubt about the product — For shoppers who browsed but didn't buy.

"Is this product worth it?" — Let other buyers answer for you.

Best for: abandoned carts, viewed products, recommendation emails.

Key changes

  • Subtitle: compressed from 2 lines to punchy one-liner
  • Each card: frames around the shopper's inner question ("Is this store legit?")
  • Added "Best for" context so merchant picks confidently
  • CTA: "Use this type" (generic) → "Add to this email" (specific action)
